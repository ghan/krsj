About the station

Radio Saint Johns is a local broadcast space for music, conversation, and neighborhood connection in Saint Johns, Oregon.

What? You don't own an FM radio?

There's one in your car. Don't have a car? Or don't want to be in your car? We get that, no worries!

Regardless of your situation, if you live in St Johns or University Park (U of Portland area), we'll drop off a small fm radio to your home for free.

The ones we want to get are good for emergencies too, you can switch to weather and emergency bands.

Just give us a shout and we'll set you up.
